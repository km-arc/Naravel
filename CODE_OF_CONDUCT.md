# Code of Conduct

Naravel is a small project that people from different countries, languages and levels of experience build together. This page
says how we treat each other wherever the project happens.

## Our pledge

We want taking part in Naravel to be free of harassment for everyone, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance,
race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

What we encourage:

- being kind and patient, above all with a newcomer's question;
- giving and accepting feedback about the work, not about the person;
- owning a mistake, apologising for it and learning from it;
- assuming good faith. Use clear, plain language; translation tools are welcome.

What we do not accept:

- harassment, threats, insults, mockery and trolling;
- sexualised language or images, and unwelcome sexual attention;
- personal or political attacks;
- publishing someone else's private information, such as an address or an email, without their permission;
- repeated unwanted contact, and anything else that would be out of place in a professional setting.

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, comments and commit messages in this repository, and to any other place where
someone speaks on behalf of the project.

## Enforcement

The maintainers may edit, hide, lock or delete a comment, close an issue or pull request, and block a person from the repository,
when that is what keeps the project a decent place to work. They decide each case by how serious it was and whether it repeated:

1. **Correction.** A private or public note saying what was out of place and why.
2. **Warning.** The same, with a stated consequence if it continues.
3. **Temporary ban.** No interaction in the repository for a set time.
4. **Permanent ban.** For a pattern of behaviour, or one act serious enough to need it.

### How to report

- On GitHub, open the menu of the comment or issue and choose **Report content**. The report goes to GitHub Support, who act
  under GitHub's own terms.
- To reach the maintainers privately, use the contact details on the project's GitHub profile (<https://github.com/km-arc>).
  If there are none, open an issue that says only that you want to raise a conduct concern, and **put no details in it**; a
  private channel will be arranged. Use clear, respectful language.

This is a small project, so we cannot promise a response time. Every report is read, handled fairly, and kept as private as the
situation allows, and nobody is punished for reporting in good faith. Maintainers who are part of a report do not decide on it.
